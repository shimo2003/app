Privacy Policy for DocScan Pro

Package Name: com.aistudio.docscanpro.zqwk
Last Updated: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DocScan Pro ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, and safeguards user information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Access and Collect

On-Device Processing: DocScan Pro processes your scanned documents locally on your device. We do not sell or upload your personal scanned documents to public servers without your direct action.
  • Camera Access (android.permission.CAMERA): Used exclusively to allow you to photograph physical documents, receipts, cards, and notes for digital conversion.
  • Storage & File Access (FileProvider): Used solely to save generated PDF files locally on your device and enable secure sharing through your chosen applications.
  • Network & Internet (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used for checking connectivity status and integrating essential third-party service features such as Google Play Services and Firebase services.

2. Third-Party Services

The app integrates services that may collect data used for authentication, diagnostics, and app performance:

  • Google Play Services: Used to provide app infrastructure and runtime dependencies.
  • Firebase (Firebase App Check & Analytics): Used for security verification and evaluating service reliability.

3. Data Retention and Security

Your documents, images, and exported files remain under your complete control within your device's local storage and designated app file paths. We implement industry-standard practices to ensure secure processing.

4. Children’s Privacy

DocScan Pro does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. You are advised to review this page periodically for any updates.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions or inquiries regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact developer support.
